Performance Analysis / Budget Planning Support

Budget Scalability & Pacing Analysis

Performance Analysis Paid Media

Context

The team needed to understand how much incremental budget could be scaled across Search, Shopping, and PMax for promotion support.

My Role

Reviewed recent performance, budget limitations, impression share, ROAS, and pacing data to recommend scalable budget ranges.

Key Actions

Assessed Search, Shopping, and PMax separately.
Identified Search had limited incremental volume due to low Lost IS due to budget.
Identified Shopping as the strongest scaling opportunity.
Identified PMax as a secondary scaling lever with likely efficiency softening.
Prepared rough spend ranges and explained expected efficiency impact.

Outcome

Recommended prioritising Shopping first, then PMax as a secondary lever, while keeping Search mostly stable.

What This Proves

Performance marketing judgement, budget pacing, scalability assessment, and ability to communicate efficiency trade-offs.

Tools & Methods

Google AdsGoogle SheetsROASImpression ShareBudget Pacing
